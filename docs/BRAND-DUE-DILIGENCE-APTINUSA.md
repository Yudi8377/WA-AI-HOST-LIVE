# Preliminary Brand Due Diligence — Aptinusa

**Review date:** 14 October 2026  
**Candidate:** Aptinusa  
**Product context:** Global multi-tenant AI business communications, customer engagement, commerce automation and SaaS workspace platform  
**Proposed tagline:** *Adaptive Intelligence. Connected Business.*

## Executive assessment

**Status: promising candidate for further screening; NOT legally cleared and not yet the final brand.**

A public-web search for the exact term **Aptinusa** did not surface a clear, prominent exact-match company or software brand in the indexed results reviewed for this preliminary pass. That is a weak positive signal only: search engines are incomplete, and it does not prove that the name is unused, that a domain is available, or that a trademark application would succeed.

The name is relatively compact and pronounceable for an Indonesian-founded global product. “Nusa” may evoke Indonesia or an archipelago, which can support origin storytelling but may also make the name feel regionally anchored. The product positioning and tagline can still communicate a global market.

## What was and was not checked

- Reviewed public search results for the exact word “Aptinusa” with company/software/AI and domain-oriented terms.
- No authoritative result in the reviewed search results confirmed the ownership or availability of `aptinusa.com` or other relevant domains.
- No definitive, class-specific official trademark clearance was obtained from the Indonesian PDKI database or WIPO Global Brand Database in this pass.
- No legal opinion, registrar/RDAP confirmation, or trademark attorney clearance was obtained.

## Required checks before adopting the name publicly

1. Search **Aptinusa**, spacing variants, phonetic variants, and visually similar names in Indonesia's official [PDKI database](https://pdki-indonesia.dgip.go.id/).
2. Search the same variants in [WIPO Global Brand Database](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database/index), then the national trademark registers of priority target markets.
3. Prioritize review of Nice classes relevant to the actual launch, especially **Class 9** (downloadable software), **Class 42** (SaaS/software and technology services), and potentially **Class 35** (business, marketing and commercial administration services). Final classes should be confirmed against the exact goods/services description.
4. Check domain registration/availability and acquisition terms through an authoritative registrar or RDAP lookup. Check at least `aptinusa.com`, relevant country domains, and practical fallback domains. Search results alone cannot establish availability.
5. Check company registries, app stores, social handles, and unregistered market use in priority countries.
6. Have a qualified trademark professional assess similarity and registrability before filing or committing substantial launch spend.

Official guidance: Indonesia's Directorate General of Intellectual Property specifically recommends checking PDKI before rebranding because identical or similar prior marks can create disputes and financial/reputational risk ([DJKI guidance](https://www.dgip.go.id/artikel/detail-artikel-berita/rebranding-usaha-cegah-sengketa-bisnis-dengan-penelusuran-merek?kategori=pengumuman)). WIPO also explains that its Global Brand Database covers multiple collections but recommends checking national/regional registers as appropriate ([WIPO database](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database/index)).

## Recommended decision

Keep **Aptinusa** as the current leading candidate for the next diligence round, with the tagline *Adaptive Intelligence. Connected Business.* Do not describe it as legally safe or fully available yet. If official searches show no material conflict and the domain/handles are obtainable on acceptable terms, it can be considered for final approval.

## Safe implementation plan

Until the owner explicitly confirms the final name after due diligence:

- Do **not** rename the GitHub repository `Yudi8377/WA-AI-HOST-LIVE`.
- Do **not** rename or recreate the Neon project, branches, database, roles, or production resources.
- Do **not** change production environment variables, URLs, authentication configuration, or customer-facing production identity.
- Do **not** purchase a domain or submit a trademark application on the owner's behalf.
- Use the candidate name only in this diligence document and internal planning artifacts.

After final approval, implement the rebrand in reversible phases: (1) app metadata and internal product copy on a branch, (2) UI/logo and docs, (3) domain and redirects only after ownership is confirmed, (4) environment/config updates with rollback plan, (5) repository rename last and only if desired, then verify auth callbacks, webhooks, CI, deployment URLs, SEO metadata and monitoring.
