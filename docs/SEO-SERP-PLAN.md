# Aptinusa SEO & SERP implementation plan

## Positioning
Primary narrative: **Adaptive Intelligence. Connected Business.** Aptinusa coordinates approved customer communication channels, CRM/business context, workflow automation and governed AI agents in one multi-tenant workspace.

## Technical SEO gates
- Unique title/description per public page; avoid indexing private dashboard, admin, API-console or preview routes.
- Add canonical URLs only after final domain and HTTPS are confirmed.
- Generate sitemap.xml and robots.txt from the final public route inventory; exclude private/app/admin internals as appropriate.
- Add Organization and SoftwareApplication structured data only for claims supported by the real product and legal business identity.
- Add Open Graph/social cards, accessible headings, descriptive internal links, image alt text and language metadata.
- Verify redirects, trailing-slash consistency, 404 handling, robots directives and canonical consistency after custom-domain setup.
- Run Lighthouse/Core Web Vitals and accessibility checks; optimize fonts, images, bundle size and mobile layout.

## Content clusters
1. AI customer service for small businesses.
2. Omnichannel customer engagement and CRM workflows.
3. Official WhatsApp Business API integration and safe automation.
4. Multi-agent AI orchestration with human oversight.
5. Connector guides only after real connector behavior is tested.
6. Security, tenant isolation, consent, audit and enterprise governance.
7. Industry playbooks and customer case studies backed by real evidence.

## SERP quality and trust
- Publish company identity, contact/support, privacy, terms, security practices and transparent product limitations.
- Use original screenshots and benchmarks from real tests; do not invent customers, ratings, results or performance metrics.
- Build backlinks only through legitimate relationships. No keyword stuffing, doorway pages, fake reviews, cloaking or automated spam.
- Publish case studies only with customer permission and verifiable measurements.

## Measurement
After domain launch, verify ownership in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools; submit sitemap; monitor indexed pages, queries, CTR, impressions, conversions, crawl errors and Core Web Vitals. Keep an SEO change log.

## Current state
This is a plan, not an SEO ranking claim. Search Console, sitemap publication on a final domain, backlink acquisition and ranking results cannot be claimed until the domain is owned and the production site is deployed and verified.
